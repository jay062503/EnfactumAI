# Compensation Review Guidelines (SAMPLE – fictional placeholder, HR only)

## 1. Review cycle
The annual compensation review runs from 1 March to 15 April. Changes take effect on 1 May.

## 2. Merit increase bands
| Performance rating | Increase band |
|---|---|
| Exceptional | 10% – 14% |
| Strong | 6% – 9% |
| Meets expectations | 3% – 5% |
| Below expectations | 0% |

## 3. Approvals
Increases above 12% or promotions require COO approval. HR Managers must record final decisions in Enfactum OS.

## 4. Confidentiality
Individual compensation data is strictly confidential and may only be discussed with the employee, their manager and People & Culture.
