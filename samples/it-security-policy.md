# IT & Information Security Policy (SAMPLE – fictional placeholder)

## 1. Accounts and passwords
- Multi-factor authentication is mandatory for Enfactum OS, email and source-code hosting.
- Passwords must be at least 14 characters and must never be shared, including with IT staff.

## 2. Devices
- Company laptops must use full-disk encryption and automatic screen lock after 5 minutes.
- Lost or stolen devices must be reported to it-security@enfactum.example within 1 hour.

## 3. Client data
- Client data may only be stored in approved systems (Enfactum OS, the company drive and approved project tools).
- Client data must never be pasted into public AI tools. Use the internal Enfactum AI Assistant instead.

## 4. Incident reporting
Report suspected phishing or security incidents immediately via the #security-incidents channel or by email.
