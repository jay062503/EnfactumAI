# Leave & Time-off Policy (SAMPLE – fictional placeholder)

## 1. Scope
Applies to all full-time employees. Contractors follow the terms of their contract.

## 2. Annual leave
- Employees accrue 20 working days of paid annual leave per calendar year, pro-rated for joiners.
- Up to 5 unused days can be carried forward to the next year and must be used by 31 March.
- Leave requests of 3 days or more must be submitted at least 10 working days in advance through Enfactum OS and approved by the reporting manager.

## 3. Sick leave
- 12 days of paid sick leave per year.
- A medical certificate is required for absences longer than 2 consecutive working days.

## 4. Parental leave
- Primary caregivers: 26 weeks paid leave.
- Secondary caregivers: 4 weeks paid leave, to be taken within 6 months of the birth or adoption.

## 5. Public holidays
The holiday calendar for each office is published in Enfactum OS at the start of each year.

## 6. Notice period and leave
Annual leave cannot be used to shorten the notice period without written approval from People & Culture.
